Ongoing experiments with pour-over technique.

## V60 recipe (current best)

- 20g coffee, 300g water (1:15 ratio)
- Water at 93°C
- Bloom: 40g for 30 seconds
- Pour in 60g increments every 30 seconds
- Total brew time: ~3 minutes

## What I've learned so far

Grind size matters more than I expected. I was grinding too coarse for months.
