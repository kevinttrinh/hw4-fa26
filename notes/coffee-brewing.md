Ongoing experiments with pour-over technique. This is exactly the kind of note
Hunch is for: half-formed, personal, not meant for anyone else.

## V60 recipe (current best)

- 20g coffee, 300g water (1:15 ratio)
- Water at 93°C
- Bloom: 40g for 30 seconds
- Pour in 60g increments every 30 seconds
- Total brew time: ~3 minutes

## What I've learned so far

Grind size matters more than I expected. I was grinding too coarse for months.
Finer grind → longer extraction → more complex flavor, up to a point.

## Things to try

- Light roast Ethiopian single origin
- 91°C vs 93°C water temperature comparison
- Turbo technique (all water at once, coarser grind)

## Verdict on gear

The cheap Hario V60 works just as well as the glass one. Don't let anyone tell
you otherwise.
